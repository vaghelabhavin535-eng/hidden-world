BLACK HOLES

Explore the mysterious regions of space where gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape.

What is a Black Hole?

A black hole is a region in space where gravity is extremely strong. Nothing, not even light, can escape from inside its event horizon.

How are They Formed?

Many black holes form when massive stars reach the end of their lives and collapse under their own gravity.

Event Horizon

The event horizon is the boundary around a black hole beyond which nothing can escape.

Types of Black Holes

Scientists classify black holes into stellar-mass, intermediate-mass and supermassive black holes.
