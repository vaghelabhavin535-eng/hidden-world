EXPLORE THE PLANETS

Discover the eight amazing planets of our solar system.

01. Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. It is also the smallest planet in our solar system.

★ Fact: A year on Mercury lasts only 88 Earth days.

02. Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It has a very thick and hot atmosphere.

★ Fact: Venus is the hottest planet in our solar system.

03. Earth

Earth is our home planet. It is the only planet known to support life.

★ Fact: About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water.

04. Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance.

★ Fact: Mars has two small moons.

05. Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system.

★ Fact: Jupiter has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot.

06. Saturn

Saturn is famous for its beautiful and large ring system.

★ Fact: Saturn is less dense than water.

07. Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant. It rotates on its side compared with most planets.

★ Fact: Uranus has a blue-green appearance.

08. Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun.

★ Fact: Neptune has extremely powerful winds.
