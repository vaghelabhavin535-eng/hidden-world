EXPLORE GALAXIES

Discover the amazing galaxies of our universe. Explore billions of stars, planets and cosmic wonders.

What is a Galaxy?

A galaxy is a huge collection of stars, planets, gas, dust and dark matter held together by gravity.

Milky Way

The Milky Way is the galaxy where our Solar System and Earth are located. It contains billions of stars.

Andromeda Galaxy

Andromeda is a large spiral galaxy and one of the nearest major galaxies to the Milky Way.

Types of Galaxies

The main types of galaxies are Spiral, Elliptical and Irregular. Each has a different shape and structure.
