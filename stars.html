STARS & NEBULAS

Discover the shining stars and beautiful clouds of gas and dust across the universe.

What are Stars?

Stars are huge glowing balls of hot gas. They produce light and heat through nuclear fusion.

What are Nebulas?

Nebulas are giant clouds of gas and dust found in space. Some are places where new stars are born.

Types of Stars

Stars have different colors and sizes. Examples include red dwarfs, yellow stars and blue giant stars.

Famous Nebulas

The Orion Nebula, Crab Nebula and Eagle Nebula are some famous nebulas in our universe.
