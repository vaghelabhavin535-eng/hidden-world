OUR SOLAR SYSTEM

Discover the Sun, eight planets and the amazing objects that make up our solar system.

What is the Solar System?

Our solar system consists of the Sun, eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids and comets.

The Sun is at the center of our solar system. Its gravity keeps the planets moving around it.

The Eight Planets

  • Mercury
  • Venus
  • Earth
  • Mars
  • Jupiter
  • Saturn
  • Uranus
  • Neptune

Interesting Fact

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system.
